## 5. Iterative Search Refinement

### Phase 1: Broad Search (Week 1)
- Execute primary search strings across all databases
- Document initial hit counts
- Perform title-level screening for relevance

### Phase 2: Focused Search (Week 2)
- Refine based on initial results
- Add specific technical terms found in relevant papers
- Search for cited and citing papers from key sources

### Phase 3: Targeted Search (Week 3)
- Author searches for prolific researchers
- Conference/journal specific searches
- Gray literature and technical reports

## 6. Search Quality Metrics

**Target Metrics:**
- Total initial results: 500-1500 papers
- After title screening: 150-300 papers
- After abstract screening: 50-100 papers
- Final full-text review: 25-50 papers

**Quality Indicators:**
- Coverage of major conferences (INTERSPEECH, ICASSP, ACL)
- Range of publication years (2015-2025)
- Mix of system papers, evaluation studies, and surveys
- Balance between academic and industry research

## 7. Backup Search Strategies

### If Initial Searches Yield Too Few Results:
- Remove "real-time" constraint, add as filter later
- Search for broader "speech translation" + "system"
- Include related terms: "interpretation", "voice translation"
- Extend date range to 2010-2025

### If Initial Searches Yield Too Many Results:
- Add more specific architecture terms
- Focus on "platform" or "framework" rather than general "system"
- Limit to high-impact venues initially
- Add performance metrics as required terms

This search strategy provides comprehensive coverage while maintaining focus on your specific research question about architectural patterns, design considerations, and performance optimization in real-time speech translation platforms.
