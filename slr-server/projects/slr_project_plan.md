# Real-time Speech Translation Platform - Systematic Literature Review Project

## Project Overview

**Title:** Real-time Speech Translation Platform Analysis and Design  
**Research Domain:** Computer Science - Natural Language Processing  
**Focus Area:** Real-time Speech Translation Systems  
**Timeline:** 16 weeks  

## Research Questions

### Primary Research Question
What are the key architectural patterns, design considerations, and performance optimization strategies for implementing effective real-time speech translation platforms?

### Secondary Research Questions
1. What are the main technical challenges in real-time speech translation?
2. Which architectural patterns are most commonly used in speech translation platforms?
3. How do different platforms handle latency optimization?
4. What are the accuracy trade-offs between speed and translation quality?
5. How do platforms scale to handle multiple concurrent users?
6. What are the best practices for handling different languages and dialects?

## PICO Framework Application

**P (Population/Problem):** Real-time speech translation platforms and systems  
**I (Intervention):** Various architectural approaches, design patterns, and optimization techniques  
**C (Comparison):** Different platform implementations, performance metrics, accuracy measures  
**O (Outcome):** System performance, translation accuracy, user experience, scalability  

## Research Scope

### Inclusion Criteria
- Academic papers on real-time speech translation systems (2015-2025)
- Technical reports on platform architectures
- Studies comparing different translation approaches
- Performance evaluation papers
- Papers on latency optimization techniques
- Multi-language support implementations

### Exclusion Criteria
- Non-real-time translation systems
- Text-only translation (without speech component)
- Papers older than 10 years (unless seminal works)
- Non-English publications without English abstracts
- Patent documents without technical details

## Screening Workflow

### Stage 1: Title and Abstract Screening
- **Reviewers:** 2 independent reviewers
- **Criteria:** Relevance to real-time speech translation
- **Agreement threshold:** κ > 0.7

### Stage 2: Full-text Review
- **Reviewers:** 2 independent reviewers  
- **Criteria:** Detailed inclusion/exclusion criteria
- **Conflict resolution:** Third reviewer consultation

### Stage 3: Quality Assessment
- **Framework:** Adapted from software engineering research quality criteria
- **Focus areas:** Technical rigor, reproducibility, evaluation methodology

## Data Extraction Plan

### Technical Information
- **Architecture patterns** (microservices, monolithic, hybrid)
- **Technology stack** (programming languages, frameworks, libraries)
- **Performance metrics** (latency, throughput, accuracy)
- **Scalability approaches** (horizontal/vertical scaling)
- **Language support** (number of languages, dialect handling)

### Evaluation Metrics
- **Translation quality** (BLEU scores, human evaluation)
- **Latency measurements** (end-to-end, component-wise)
- **Resource usage** (CPU, memory, bandwidth)
- **User experience metrics** (satisfaction, usability)

## Quality Assurance

### Inter-rater Reliability
- Cohen's Kappa calculation for screening agreements
- Regular calibration meetings between reviewers
- Clear decision criteria documentation

### Risk Mitigation
- **Publication bias:** Include grey literature and technical reports
- **Language bias:** Include non-English papers with English abstracts
- **Recency bias:** Balance recent innovations with established methods
